# 📱 Install PuraTech Store on Your Phone

PuraTech Store is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that you can install on your phone like a native app!

## 🌟 Benefits of Installing

- 📲 **Works like a native app** - Icon on your home screen
- ⚡ **Faster loading** - Optimized performance
- 📴 **Works offline** - Browse products even without internet
- 🔔 **Full screen** - No browser bars, more space
- 🎯 **Quick access** - Launch directly from home screen

---

## 📱 iPhone / iPad (iOS & iPadOS)

### Step-by-Step Installation

1. **Open Safari Browser**
   - Open Safari (must use Safari, not Chrome)
   - Navigate to: **https://puratech-pwa.pages.dev**

2. **Tap the Share Button**
   - Look for the share icon at the bottom of the screen
   - It looks like a box with an arrow pointing up 📤

3. **Scroll Down**
   - In the share menu, scroll down until you see:
   - **"Add to Home Screen"** option

4. **Add to Home Screen**
   - Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
   - You can customize the name (default: "PuraTech Store")
   - Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner

5. **Done! 🎉**
   - You'll see the PuraTech Store icon on your home screen
   - Tap it to open the app
   - It will open in full screen without Safari's browser bars

### Screenshots Location

**Share Button:**
- iPhone with Home Button: Bottom center
- iPhone without Home Button: Top right (share icon)

## 🤖 Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, etc.)

### Method 1: Automatic Prompt (Easiest)

1. **Open the Website**
   - Open Chrome browser
   - Navigate to: **https://puratech-pwa.pages.dev**

2. **Wait for Install Prompt**
   - After a few seconds, you'll see a banner at the bottom
   - "Add PuraTech Store to Home screen"

3. **Tap "Install"**
   - Tap the **"Install"** button
   - Confirm when prompted

4. **Done! 🎉**
   - The app will be installed on your home screen
   - You can launch it like any other app

### Method 2: Manual Installation (Chrome)

1. **Open Chrome**
   - Navigate to: **https://puratech-pwa.pages.dev**

2. **Open Chrome Menu**
   - Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner

3. **Add to Home Screen**
   - Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**
   - You might need to scroll in the menu

4. **Confirm**
   - Tap **"Add"** or **"Install"**

5. **Done! 🎉**
   - Find the app icon on your home screen

### Method 3: Samsung Internet

1. **Open Samsung Internet**
   - Navigate to: **https://puratech-pwa.pages.dev**

2. **Menu**
   - Tap the menu button (three lines ☰)

3. **Add to Home Screen**
   - Tap **"Add page to"**
   - Select **"Home screen"**

4. **Done! 🎉**

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### iOS Issues

**"Add to Home Screen" option not showing?**
- ✅ Make sure you're using **Safari** browser (not Chrome)
- ✅ Check iOS version (iOS 11.3 or higher required)
- ✅ Try reloading the page

**App doesn't open in full screen?**
- ✅ Make sure you opened it from the home screen icon
- ✅ Delete and reinstall the app

### Android Issues

**Install prompt doesn't appear?**
- ✅ Clear browser cache and cookies
- ✅ Make sure you're using Chrome or supported browser
- ✅ Try the manual method instead

**Can't find "Add to Home screen"?**
- ✅ Update Chrome to the latest version
- ✅ Check if browser supports PWA
- ✅ Try a different browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet)

## 🔄 Uninstalling the App

### iOS
1. Long-press the app icon on home screen
2. Tap **"Remove App"**
3. Select **"Delete App"**

### Android
1. Long-press the app icon
2. Drag to **"Uninstall"** or tap **"App info"**
3. Tap **"Uninstall"**

## 📞 Need Help?

If you have trouble installing:
